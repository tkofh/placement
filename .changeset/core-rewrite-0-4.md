Rewrite the core around an exact-arithmetic numerical contract and a redesigned API.

**Precision (PRECISION.md).** Constructions are exact IEEE 754: the stored `precision` concept and per-constructor rounding are gone everywhere (factories, `rect.from*`, `setPrecision`, `precision` ops). Predicates are tolerance-aware instead: every value type gains `equals`, and `Interval`/`Rect` gain `contains`/`intersects`, all scaled to the data via `curvy/number`. `pipe`/`dual`/`Pipeable` now come from `curvy` (new dependency).

**Renames.** The canonical style is now Effect-style namespace imports (`import * as Interval from 'placement/interval'`): every factory is `make` (`Interval.make(0, 100)` replaces `interval(0, 100)`), factory statics are plain module exports (`Rect.zero`, `Rect.fromInterval`, `Point.infinity`, `Offset.xy` replace `rect.zero`, `point.infinity`, ...), and `applyFlexbox` is `Flexbox.apply`. Also: `dimensions`/`offset` bare-noun setters are now `set*`; `mix`/`polarMix` are `lerp`; `frame.setSize`/`setMinSize`/`setMaxSize` take `(width, height?)` (width first); `rect.shrinkByOffset`/`growByOffset` are `contract`/`expand` (and accept plain numbers); `offset.invert` is `negate`; `frame.stretchCross` is `stretch`; point's `delta` is `subtract` and its `r`/`theta` getters are the `magnitude`/`angle` functions. Angles are radians (atan2 convention).

**Removed.** Offset's string-keyed `set()` and its 8 `min*`/`max*` ops; rect's 8 `min*`/`max*` ops, fixed `align*` variants, `adjust*` family, and `setTop`/`setLeft` aliases; dimensions' `scaleX`/`scaleY` and `aspectRatio.fixWidth`/`fixHeight`; point's polar setters, corner ops, `arcLength`, and raw `r`/`theta`/`x`/`y` helpers; the dead `Frame.justify`/`stretchMain` options; the root entrypoint (subpath exports only); the `placement/utils` subpath (`pipe`, `flow`, and `dual` come from `curvy/utils`, and every value has a `.pipe` method); CommonJS output (ESM only).

**Added.** `interval` `join`/`intersect`/`min`/`max`; point `add`/`scale`/`min`/`max`; rect `lerp`/`scaleFrom`/`center`/`isRectLike`; `isPointLike`/`isDimensionsLike`; exported `FrameInput`/`FlexboxInput`/`FlexDirection`/`FlexWrap`/`StackItem`/`SequenceItem` types; `flexbox.stretchItems` now actually works (container default for per-item `stretch`, inherit via non-finite sentinel); frames keep unset sizing through setter chains; JSDoc on the entire public surface.

**Fixed.** `interval()` returned a NaN-sized interval; `dimensions.cover` was wrong for all non-square inputs; `rect.fromDimensions` leaked its precision argument into `x`; `track.remap`'s two-argument form returned a function; `interval.avoid` could leave the interval overlapping the forbidden zone; polar angles broke beyond one negative revolution; every multi-argument data-last `frame` setter constructed garbage; rect's curried clamp overloads produced NaN rects; main-axis auto margins leaked `Infinity` positions; a lone overflowing frame in a wrapping container was laid out twice; `stack` dropped definite offsets beside auto offsets, truncated overflowing items below their `min`, and let auto offsets go negative; `sequence` let `place` shift auto-margin layouts and resolved auto offsets negative on overflow; fractional `growRatio`/`shrinkRatio` misreported consumed space, misplacing `place`/`space`; grow/shrink distribution no longer chases float dust; the wrap decision and all boundary predicates are tolerance-aware and scale-invariant; non-finite fields compare exactly in `equals` (an infinity equals only itself) and infinite bounds no longer blow up the `contains`/`intersects` band; frame offsets are physical in reversed flows (a `columnReverse` `offsetTop` stays at the physical top); a wrapping item's cross-axis `min` no longer inflates the previous line; crossed clamp bounds resolve min-wins (CSS semantics) everywhere.
