Merged lanes move with their routes. The routes of a merge come in where
their whisks start, on any line that meets the merged side's clearance
there within reach of the port, so a route held off the port's line runs
straight in rather than jog onto it, as long as its step onto the port's
line stays an S of the whisk's radius with room for the other lanes;
otherwise it jogs in its own run. The whisk reaches far enough out for
every such S at full radius. Other routes cross the runs a merge's
routes come in on rather than ride along or between them. The nudge
spreads the routes onto lanes the merge gap apart, in the order the
routes part in: centered on the line where nothing is in the way, and
shifted together where something is, so a lane makes room for a route
beside it rather than hold a planned spot. A jog right behind a lane
stops a gap short of another route in its way rather than cross it or
press its lane aside. Each lane's whisk steps onto the line from where
its lane lands. A merged side's clearance deepens to hold the whisks, so
a route along that side runs on one face, the gap from it where it
crosses the lanes, instead of around a zone that stuck out past the
clearance; a zone over the lanes still walls off the whisks where the
deeper clearance would reach into a neighbor's, cover a port or a
waypoint, or close a passage on a route.
